The Kerala Ministry refers to the group of government departments in Kerala that manage the development and administration of the state. These ministries work under the Chief Minister and help provide public services to the people .


Different ministries handle different sectors such as tourism, education, healthcare, transport, agriculture, finance, and public welfare. Their main aim is to improve infrastructure, education, healthcare, tourism, and the quality of life of the people in Kerala..

Tourism Ministry

Promotes Kerala tourism, backwaters, beaches, hill stations, and eco-tourism.

Education Ministry

Works to improve schools, higher education,literacy, and digital learning in Kerala.

Health Ministry

Provides healthcare services, hospitals, Ayurveda support, and wellness programs.

Development Ministry

Focuses on transport, infrastructure, public welfare, and state development.
