Kerala is one of the most beautiful states in southern India. It is widely known for its greenery, backwaters, beaches, hill stations, and rich cultural traditions. Because of its natural beauty, Kerala is often called “God’s Own Country.” Tourism plays a major role in the state’s economy, attracting visitors from all over the world to places like Munnar, Alleppey, Wayanad, Kochi, and Thekkady. .


The government of Kerala is managed through different ministries that handle areas such as tourism, education, health, finance, transport, and public welfare. The head of the Kerala government is the Chief Minister, currently V D Satheeshan. The ministries work together to improve development, tourism, infrastructure, and public services across the state.
